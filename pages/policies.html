Funeral arrangements and quotes

We explain every choice and every cost before you decide. Take your time, and ask us anything.

Your quote is an estimate

  • A quote is our best estimate of the price. It is based on the choices you give us.
  • You can ask for a quote on our Get a quote page, by phone, or at our office.
  • A quote is not a bill. You do not pay anything to get one.
  • Our quotes are in Barbados dollars (Bds$).
  • Our own prices in a quote stay the same for 30 days.
  • Charges from others can change at any time. If they do, we tell you first.

What can change the price

  • Choices you make later. For example, a different casket, more hymn sheets, or extra announcements.
  • Charges from others. For example, the church, the cemetery, the crematorium, the pathologist, certificates and permits, and announcements on CBC or in the Nation newspaper.
  • When a loved one travels overseas: airline freight, customs, and the funeral home in the other country.
  • Others may change their charges after we give you the quote.

If the price will change, we tell you first. We confirm the final price with you before we send the invoice.

Charges from others, for every funeral

  • Charges from others are passed on to you at the price they charge us. We do not add to them.
  • For example, the church, the cemetery, the crematorium, the pathologist, permits and certificates, and announcements on CBC or in the Nation newspaper.
  • When a loved one travels: airline freight, customs, and the funeral home in the other country.
  • They are shown on your invoice.
  • If they change, we tell you before we go ahead.

What we need to start

  • A call from the family, at any hour, on {{PHONE}}.
  • Where your loved one is now. For example, at home, in hospital or in a nursing home.
  • The name, address, phone number and email of the person making the arrangements.
  • The papers we ask for, so we can get the certificates and permits. We tell you which ones.
  • A meeting at our office to choose the arrangements. We write them down, with the price, for you to check.

The person who makes the arrangements with us is responsible for paying the invoice.

Paying for our services

We keep paying simple. You always know what you owe, and when.

Your invoice

  • When the arrangements are agreed, we send you an invoice.
  • It comes as a secure link, by email or WhatsApp. You do not need an account or a password to open it.
  • The link shows how much you have paid, and how much is still owed.
  • The invoice shows the date it is due. This is usually 14 days after the date of the invoice.

Deposits

  • We may ask for a deposit before the funeral. It helps cover the costs we pay to others for you.
  • We tell you the amount when you make the arrangements.
  • The deposit is taken off what you owe. Your invoice shows it as paid.

Ways to pay

  • At our office at Bush Hall: cash, cheque, or card (Visa or Mastercard) on our CIBC FirstCaribbean card machine.
  • By cheque: please make it payable to Two Sons Funeral Home Ltd.
  • By bank transfer: our bank details are on your invoice. Please use the invoice number as the reference.
  • Online by card, once online payments start: your invoice link will have a button to pay by Visa or Mastercard. You pay on the secure page of our payment provider. See Paying by card and keeping it safe.
  • Paying online pays the full amount still owed on that invoice. To pay part of it, for example a deposit, ask us for a payment link for that amount.
  • All payments are in Barbados dollars (Bds$).

Payment plans

  • If you cannot pay all at once, please talk to us. We can agree a payment plan with you.
  • We write the plan down, with the amounts and the dates.
  • While you keep to the plan, we pause our reminder emails.

Payment reminders

  • If an invoice is still open, we may send you a short, gentle reminder by email.
  • The first one comes a few days before the due date. Others may follow on the due date and after it.
  • We send at most one a day, and none on Sundays.
  • Each reminder has a link to your invoice.
  • Need more time? Call us, and we can pause the reminders.
  • Already paid? Thank you. Please call us if a reminder still arrives, and we will check.

We do not charge interest or late fees.

Receipts

  • We give you a receipt for every payment you make at our office.
  • After you pay online by card, we email you a receipt.
  • If we do not have your email address, the payment page tells you. Please keep that page, or take a screenshot of it, as your receipt.

If you pay too much

  • This can happen, for example, if you pay at our office and also online.
  • We tell you, and we refund the difference. A card payment is refunded to the same card.
  • Or, if you ask us to, we put it towards another invoice of yours.

Paying by card and keeping it safe

  • After we confirm your order or arrangements, we send you a secure link to your invoice. You can pay it at our office or, once online payments start, online by card.
  • When you pay by card online, you type your card details on the secure payment page of our payment provider. It is a payment gateway approved by our bank, CIBC FirstCaribbean.
  • Your card details go straight to the payment provider, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS, using TLS). Before you type them, check for the padlock beside the web address, and that the address starts with “https”.
  • When you pay online, Two Sons never sees or keeps your full card number or security code.
  • We keep only the result of the payment: the amount, the date, the payment reference, the card type, the last four digits and the bank’s approval code.
  • Our payment provider meets the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS). These are the security rules set by the card companies.
  • Your bank may ask you to confirm that it is really you, with Visa Secure or Mastercard Identity Check (also called 3-D Secure). This may be a code sent to your phone, or a check in your banking app.
  • At our office, you pay by card on the CIBC FirstCaribbean card machine. We do not write down your card number.
  • Card payments are charged in Barbados dollars (BBD).

Please keep your card number safe. Never send it by email, WhatsApp or text message, or in the forms on this website, and never read it out on the phone. Our staff will never ask for your card number.

Refunds and cancellations

We know plans can change at a difficult time. This is how cancellations and refunds work.

Floral tributes

  • You can cancel a floral tribute by phone or email, up to 24 hours before the funeral.
  • You get a full refund, as long as we have not started making it.
  • After that, we cannot give a refund. Each tribute is made to order, just for you.
  • If we did not deliver your tribute, or it was not what you ordered, please tell us. We will give you a refund or a replacement.

Funeral services and goods

  • If arrangements are cancelled, we refund what you paid for services and goods we have not yet provided. This includes any deposit.
  • We keep back:
    • the cost of services and goods we have already provided, for example removal, keeping of the body, or dressing and preparation;
    • costs we have already paid to others for you, for example church or cemetery fees, permits, or newspaper notices;
    • items ordered specially for you, once we have ordered them (see Caskets, coffins and urns).
  • Plans made ahead: see Changing or cancelling a plan.
  • Livestreams: see Livestreams.

How we pay refunds

  • We send your refund within 14 days of agreeing it with you.
  • Card payments are refunded to the same card. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show the refund.
  • Other payments are refunded by cheque or bank transfer.
  • If you pay more than you owe, we refund the difference.

How to ask for a refund

Call us on {{PHONE}}, or email info@twosonsfuneralhome.com. Please tell us your name, your loved one’s name, and what you would like to cancel.

Planning ahead

Planning ahead is your choice. You can plan as much or as little as you like. Talking to us about it costs nothing.

What a plan records

  • Your wishes. For example, burial or cremation, the church, the hymns, the flowers and the cars.
  • Your name and contact details, and the family members you want us to contact.
  • An estimate of the price, at today’s prices, if you ask for one.
  • Any payments you make towards the plan.

We give you a copy of your plan, and keep a copy safely at our office.

Paying towards a plan

  • You do not have to pay ahead.
  • If you want to, you can pay towards your plan, in amounts we agree with you.
  • We record every payment on your plan, and give you a receipt each time.
  • You can ask us for a statement of what you have paid, at any time.
  • Payments towards your plan are used only for your funeral with us, unless you cancel the plan.

Prices can change

  • Your estimate uses today’s prices.
  • By the time of the funeral, our prices may have changed.
  • So may the charges of others, such as the church, the cemetery, the crematorium, the newspaper and the airlines.
  • The final price is worked out at the time of the funeral, at the prices then. Everything you have paid is taken off.

Changing or cancelling a plan

  • You can change your wishes at any time. Just call or visit us.
  • You can cancel your plan at any time, by phone, email or letter.
  • When you cancel, we refund what you paid towards the plan. We keep back only costs we have already paid to others for you, and items ordered specially for you.
  • We pay the refund in the same way as other refunds (see How we pay refunds).
  • When the time comes, your family may choose another funeral home. If so, we refund what you paid to your estate, less any costs we have already paid to others for you.

When the time comes

  • Your family calls us, at any hour. We follow your plan as closely as we can.
  • If something in your plan is no longer possible, we talk to your family about the nearest choice.

Cremation, repatriation and burial at sea

These services need extra permits and papers. Some of the timing is decided by others. This is what to expect.

Permits and papers

  • Cremation, burial at sea, and sending a loved one overseas all need permits from the authorities in Barbados.
  • Bringing a loved one home, or sending them overseas, also needs papers for the other country and the airline.
  • We apply for the permits for you. We tell you which papers we need from you, for example certificates and identity documents.
  • We cannot fix the date until the permits are granted.

Timing

  • Some of the timing depends on others: the authorities, the crematorium, the airlines, customs, the funeral home in the other country, and the weather.
  • We do everything we can to keep to your plans. But we cannot control delays caused by others.
  • If anything changes, we tell you straight away. We keep you informed at every step.

Charges from others

Charges from others, such as the crematorium, permits, airline freight and customs, are passed on at the price they charge us. See Charges from others, for every funeral.

Cremation

  • We arrange the cremation, the service, the certificates and the hearse.
  • We tell you when the ashes are ready, and how to collect them.

Repatriation

  • We bring loved ones home to Barbados, and send them overseas, with the permits that Barbados and the other country require.
  • We handle the paperwork, customs and freight.
  • The flight date depends on the airline, and on the permits.

Burial at sea

  • A burial at sea depends on the weather and the sea.
  • If it is not safe to go out, we agree a new day with you.
  • Please call us to talk through what is involved.

Your privacy

We treat your personal details with the same care we give your family. This is what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do.

What we collect

  • Your name, address, phone number and email address.
  • The details of your order or quote request, or of the funeral arrangements you make with us.
  • Your wishes, if you plan ahead with us.
  • Invoices and payments: what you owe, what you paid, when, and how. For a cheque or bank transfer, the cheque number or bank reference.
  • For an online card payment, only the result: the payment reference, the amount, the card type, the last four digits and the bank’s approval code.
  • A record of the invoice, receipt and reminder emails we send you, and when we sent them.
  • The details a family gives us for an obituary: the funeral flyer, the photo, the family names and the service details.
  • Condolence messages and feedback you send us.

We never keep your full card number or security code. Our website forms never ask for card details.

Why we use it

  • To arrange funeral services and fill your orders.
  • To send you invoices, receipts and payment reminders, to take payment, and to give refunds.
  • To answer your messages and quote requests.
  • To keep your plan, if you plan ahead with us.
  • To publish obituaries and livestream links, with the family’s agreement.
  • To pass condolence messages to the family, with your name and any email address you give.
  • To keep our business and tax records.

Who else handles it

We share your details only with the people and services that help us care for you, and only what they need:

  • Tech Nova Barbados Limited, our IT provider. It:
    • runs our back-office system, for invoices, payments, reminders and obituaries;
    • keeps customer names, contact details, invoices and payments for us;
    • shows you your invoice and payment pages;
    • sends our invoice, receipt and reminder emails, through an email delivery service;
    • looks after this website and our email.
  • Our payment provider, a payment gateway approved by our bank, takes online card payments on its own secure page. CIBC FirstCaribbean, our bank, handles card payments, online and on our office card machine.
  • Anthropic (its Claude AI service). If we switch on automatic flyer reading, a photo of the funeral flyer is sent to it, to help us type the obituary. Our staff check every detail.
  • The people who help with a funeral you arrange with us. For example, the church, the cemetery, the crematorium, the newspaper or radio for announcements, government offices for certificates and permits, and, for repatriation, the airline and the funeral home in the other country.
  • Our streaming partners, when you choose to have a service streamed.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the forms on this website to our email.
  • Microsoft 365 is our email service.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, it sees your internet address when a page opens. The website’s files, including obituaries and published condolences, are kept on GitHub.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (run by Cloudflare) supply the lettering and page styles. Your browser connects to them when a page opens, so they see your internet address.
  • Google Maps shows the map on our Contact page.
  • Livestream links, and the WhatsApp and Facebook share buttons, open other services only when you choose them. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Some of these services are based outside Barbados, for example in the United States. So your details may be stored or handled in other countries.

We never sell your details, and we do not use them for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Funeral arrangement records, invoices, payments and the record of emails we sent: for as long as Barbados law requires us to keep business and tax records.
  • Plans made ahead: while the plan is open. After the funeral, as for other funeral records.
  • Website messages, quote requests and flower orders: up to two years, unless they become part of a funeral arrangement.
  • Obituaries, their flyer photos and published condolence messages: while the obituary is on our website. The family member who made the arrangements can ask us to take an obituary down at any time.

Your rights

Under the Barbados Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask us:

  • to see the personal details we hold about you;
  • to correct them, if they are wrong;
  • to delete them, when the law does not require us to keep them.

How to ask about or delete your details

  • Email info@twosonsfuneralhome.com, write to us, or call us and we will help.
  • Tell us your name, and what you would like us to do.
  • We may ask you to show who you are. This keeps your details safe.
  • We reply within 30 days.
  • When we delete your details, we delete them from our email, our back-office system and our website.
  • Our website is kept on GitHub. Old copies of a page can stay there for a while after we take it down. If you ask, we will have them removed too.
  • Some records we must keep by law, such as invoices and payment records. If we cannot delete something, we tell you why.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Data Protection Commissioner of Barbados.

Cookies and browser storage

  • This website does not set its own cookies. It does not use tracking or advertising tools.
  • The Flowers page keeps your basket on your own device until you send your order or close the tab.
  • The Google map on our Contact page may set Google’s own cookies.
  • Livestream services, such as YouTube, may set their own cookies when you open a stream.
  • Our payment provider’s secure payment page may use its own cookies, to keep your payment safe.
