Planning ahead

A thoughtful decision. A lasting gift.

Pre-planning is a chance to talk through your wishes, understand the choices involved and write down what your family will need to know. A conversation with us helps you decide how much planning feels right.

1

Start a conversation

Speak privately with us at Bush Hall, or by phone, about what pre-planning involves.

2

Think about your wishes

Burial or cremation, the church, the hymns, the flowers, the cars, and the details that matter to you.

3

Write down the key details

Write down your wishes and share them with your family. We can help you think through what to include.

4

Review when you need to

Look at your wishes again whenever your circumstances change.

Common questions

Pre-planning, explained simply.

Do I need to make every decision now?

No. A first meeting can simply help you understand the choices. You decide how much you want to write down.

Can my family be involved?

Yes. You can bring trusted family members with you, and share your wishes with them.

Can I get an idea of the cost?

Yes. Use our Get a quote form to choose burial, cremation, shipment or a memorial service, and we will reply with a price.
