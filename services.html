Our services

Everything a family needs, arranged by one team at Bush Hall. Call us any time, or get a quote online.

Complete funeral arrangements

From the first call to the final farewell, we take care of everything: removal, dressing and preparation, the casket, church, graveside and cemetery arrangements, announcements on CBC and in the Nation, hymn sheets, prayer cards, buttons, condolence books, flowers and transport. You tell us your wishes and we handle the details.

Get a quote for a burial

Chapel & viewings

Our Tranquility Chapel at Bush Hall is on site for viewings and services, so your family can gather in a calm, private space.

Live streaming

We can arrange a live stream of the service, so family near and far can watch together. The link goes on the funeral notice, ready to share on WhatsApp.

See livestreams

Cremation

Cremation with a service, so family and friends can say goodbye. We arrange the cremation case, the service, the certificates and the hearse, with the same care as any funeral.

Get a quote for a cremation

Repatriation

We bring loved ones home to Barbados, and send them overseas to be laid to rest with family. We handle the paperwork, customs and freight, and keep you informed at every step.

Get a quote for repatriation

Burial at sea

For those who loved the sea, we can arrange a burial at sea. Call us to talk through what is involved and how we can make the day meaningful.

Memorial services

A service to remember a life, at a church or venue of your choice. We can arrange the hymn sheets and a livestream for family abroad.

Get a quote for a memorial service

Limousine & taxi

Our Cadillac hearses and limousines carry your loved one and your family with dignity on the funeral day. Our taxi service is there for family and guests too.
