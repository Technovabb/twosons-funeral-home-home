What to do first

You do not need to know the steps. We walk you through each one.

1

Call us, day or night

Ring (246) 426-1205 at any hour. We tell you exactly what happens next.

2

We bring your loved one into our care

Our team comes to the home, hospital or nursing home with dignity and respect.

3

Plan the service together

Meet us at Bush Hall to choose the service, the notice, the flowers and the cars.

4

Share the notice

We post the funeral notice and livestream link here, ready to share on WhatsApp.
